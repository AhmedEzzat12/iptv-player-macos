# Tuner — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Tuner is a media player for IPTV playlists you add yourself. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
tracking, and the developer receives no data from it.

**What stays on your device.** Your playlists (including any usernames and passwords), the TV guide, watch history,
favourites, downloads and settings are stored on your device only.

**iCloud sync (optional, off by default).** If you turn it on, Tuner keeps a copy of your favourites, watch progress,
settings and playlists (without passwords) in a folder in your own iCloud Drive. Apple stores it under your Apple
Account; the developer has no access to it.

**Requests Tuner makes for you.**
- To your IPTV provider: the playlists, guides and streams you add.
- Optional online details (Settings → Metadata, on by default): artwork and descriptions from Cinemeta, or TMDB if
  you add your own key, looked up by the title's name or public IDs.
- Optional Skip Intro timings: TheIntroDB and IntroDB, asked with the title's public IMDb/TMDB ID and the season and
  episode number only.
- Optional Top 10 rows (off by default): public weekly popularity charts, downloaded and matched with your library on
  the device.
- Trailers play from YouTube when you ask for one.

None of these requests include your playlists, your provider, your credentials or what you watch. Each of these
services has its own privacy policy.

**On-device AI features** (off by default) run entirely on your device.

**Children.** Tuner isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

**Contact.** Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/AhmedEzzat12/iptv-player-macos/issues.
